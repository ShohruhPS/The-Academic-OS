How GPA is calculated

Every course percentage is converted to grade points using a scale. Your unweighted GPA is the simple average of those points. Your weighted GPA gives each course a weight (credits, or lessons per week) so bigger courses count for more.

Weighted GPA = (points₁ × weight₁ + points₂ × weight₂ + …) ÷ (weight₁ + weight₂ + …)

Example: Maths 92% (4.0 points, weight 5) and History 68% (3.0 points, weight 3). Unweighted GPA = (4.0 + 3.0) ÷ 2 = 3.50. Weighted GPA = (4.0×5 + 3.0×3) ÷ (5+3) = 29 ÷ 8 = 3.63.

Using this for IGCSE, A-Level, AP and IB

IGCSE, A-Level, AP and IB are graded with letters or scores (for example 1–7 in the IB and 1–5 in AP) rather than a GPA, so schools and universities often convert marks to a 4.0 scale. This calculator works from your percentage marks, and you can switch scales to match the conversion your school uses. Always confirm the official conversion with your school or the university you are applying to.

GPA calculator FAQ

How do I convert a percentage to GPA?

Find the range your percentage falls in on the conversion table and read off the grade points. With the default scale, 90–100% is 4.0, 80–89% is 3.7, 70–79% is 3.3, 60–69% is 3.0 and 50–59% is 2.0.

What is the difference between weighted and unweighted GPA?

Unweighted GPA is the plain average of your course points. Weighted GPA gives each course a weight, such as credits or lessons per week, so courses with a bigger weight affect the result more.

What counts as a good GPA?

It depends on your school and goals. Many universities look for roughly 3.0 or higher, and competitive programmes often expect 3.5 or above, but requirements vary, so check the specific institution.

Which scale should I choose?

Choose the scale your school or university uses. If you are unsure, use the default scale and confirm the official conversion with your school.

Is my data saved or shared?

The calculation runs entirely in your browser. Your entries are kept on your own device so you can come back later, and they are not sent to our servers.
