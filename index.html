FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is The Academic OS?

It is a free web app that puts a student's grades, GPA, tasks, notes, timetable, revision plan and focus timer in one place. It works in your browser on any device, so there is nothing to install.

Is it really free?

Yes. Creating an account and using every tool is free.

Which curricula and grading systems does it support?

It is built with IGCSE, A-Level (AS/A2), AP, IB and national curricula in mind. You set how many assessments a subject has and how much tests count against the final exam, and you can change the percentage-to-GPA scale to match your school.

Is it available in Russian and Uzbek?

Notes can be translated between English, Russian and Uzbek, and this website is available in all three languages.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored in your account in the cloud so it syncs across devices, and a copy is kept in your browser. You can export a backup at any time.

How is GPA calculated?

Each subject's percentage is converted to grade points using a scale you can edit (by default 90% and above is 4.0, 80% is 3.7, 70% is 3.3, 60% is 3.0 and 50% is 2.0). Unweighted GPA is the plain average, and weighted GPA weights each subject by its lessons per week.
